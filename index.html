Driving & parking

Church parking is available on a first-come, first-served basis. To enter the church car park, follow the one-way road to the church entrance on Upper Changi Road.

If the church car park is full, you can park at ITE College East opposite, subject to available spaces. Please note that parking at ITE is chargeable.
